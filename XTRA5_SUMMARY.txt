Xtra5 - feature list (version 1.35.0)
Everything official Xtra does, plus the features below.

========================================
Always up to date
- Stays current with official Xtra: new versions arrive automatically and install with one tap
  from inside the app.
- Installs alongside official Xtra, so you can keep both.
- See what's new in official Xtra right from Settings.

========================================
Chat
- Highlight the people you care about: give any chatter's messages a colored background.
  - Organize them into named groups (for example Mods or Friends), each with its own color.
  - Change a color, rename, move or remove anyone in a couple of taps, and see the result live
    in the chat while you choose.
  - Open your highlights straight from the video player; in landscape the panels sit beside
    the chat and use the extra width.
  - Pick a default group, then long-press anyone in chat to add them to it (or take them out).
- A full color picker: color wheel, hex codes, brightness and transparency, presets and your
  own saved colors, with a realistic chat preview.
- Color-code special messages: subs and announcements, first-time chatters, channel point
  rewards, mentions of you and bot messages.
  - Common bots are recognized out of the box, and you can add your own.
- Make chat your own: message spacing, separator lines, font choice and username size.
- Animated emotes play in sync across the whole chat.
- Tap an emote to see its details; tap a message to see the conversation.
- Long-press an emote in chat to put it in your message, ready to send.
- Conversation and emote popups open beside the chat, so the video stays visible.
- Compact reply lines that show more of the original message.

========================================
Watching
- Built for TV remotes (also keyboards and game controllers):
  - Left / Right to skip back or forward by your chosen amount, with an on-screen indicator.
  - Up to scrub through the video, by tapping or holding Left / Right, then OK to jump.
  - Down for chapters, hold OK to reload a stream, Back to close a minimized video.
- Settings button right in the player, and playback picks up where it left off when you return.
- Battery percentage in fullscreen that fades out of the way.
- Live streams keep playing when your earbuds disconnect or go in their case, and resuming a
  live stream always jumps back to live.
- Tap a paused mini player to resume it.

========================================
Browsing
- Favourite streamers: long-press a profile picture to add a gold ring and star. Favourites
  appear first in your Live list, with everyone else in alphabetical order.
- Long-press a stream to open it audio-only.
- Clean stream cards: streamer, category and title, with text sizes you can adjust.
- Layouts that stay readable on big screens and TVs with lots of columns.
- Refresh everything with one button (or just tap the Live tab), and lists refresh themselves
  when you come back to the app.
- Optional fixed top bar that stays in place while you scroll.
- Back always takes you home to your Live list before leaving the app.
- Press Following again from its main page to jump straight to a freshly refreshed Live list.
